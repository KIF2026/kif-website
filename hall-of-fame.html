Hall of Fame

Recognizing enduring legacy, character, and contribution.

In Memoriam

Sensei Miguel Vélez

Sensei Miguel Vélez, though he passed before the founding of the Kenpo International Federation, remains a foundational figure in its spirit and legacy. He made significant contributions to North Hollywood Kenpo Karate, an institution that would later evolve into KIF.

At his wake, Jorge Mario Calvillo expressed the depth of their bond by saying, “You were not just a friend, you were my master,” a powerful reflection of Sensei Miguel’s influence and character.

Beyond martial arts, Sensei Miguel Vélez was known as a devoted son, husband, father, and friend whose impact continues to be remembered by those who had the privilege of knowing him.

Hall of Fame

Sensei Rigoberto Samayoa

Sensei Rigoberto Samayoa is honored as an active inductee into the KIF Hall of Fame. A distinguished martial artist, he built an important legacy in Guatemala and played a pivotal role in Jorge Mario Calvillo’s development by awarding him his first black belt in Kenpo Karate.

A 7th-degree black belt in American Kenpo, Sensei Samayoa continues to represent the art with excellence while residing in Canada.

He is also recognized as a devoted son, husband, father, friend, and sensei whose character and citizenship reflect the values that KIF seeks to honor.

Hall of Fame

Sensei Mohamad Tabatabai

Sensei Mohamad Tabatabai is honored as a distinguished inductee into the KIF Hall of Fame. He earned his black belt directly from Grand Master Ed Parker and went on to become one of the most influential figures in American Kenpo.

He directed multiple martial arts schools under Grand Master Parker and later founded his own schools across the Los Angeles area. Internationally recognized, he has taught seminars around the world and has also contributed to the evolution of the art through his American Kenpo Jiu-Jitsu system.

Sensei Tabatabai also played an important role in the journey of Jorge Mario Calvillo by recognizing and authenticating his black belt and later awarding his rank advancements through 9th degree.
